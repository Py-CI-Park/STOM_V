## P1 OOS-Blindness Check

timestamp=2026-06-04T12:33:00+09:00

The selector is fixed before fresh 2022/2026 OOS execution.

Allowed selector inputs are restricted to training-generation fields listed in `p1-selector-spec.md` and `p1-selector-spec.json`.

Forbidden selector inputs:
- 2022 OOS metrics
- 2026 OOS metrics
- slippage-stress outputs
- PBO/DSR outputs
- final decision verdicts
- post-OOS analysis fields

Prior run replay is allowed only with `diagnostic_only=true`; it validates mechanics and cannot support an efficacy claim.

Any selected candidate must be frozen to an artifact with `oos_excluded=true` before P5 starts.
