## Goals

- Use the Wide v1 CSV as the baseline result input for `discovery research`.
- Generate a candidate expression pool from the 40,937-trade CSV.
- Apply `estimated_retention` filtering before candidate backtests.
- Execute one round of candidate backtests with `candidate_count=5`.
- Rank candidates with retention-penalized ranking.
- Identify `best_candidate`.
- Distinguish `best_candidate` from a promotion-ready candidate.
- Analyze promotion failure reasons.
- Decide whether the next step is:
  - promote/WFO validation,
  - candidate generation/gate tuning,
  - or multi-round improvement loop v2.

## Non-Goals

- Do not run WFO inside `discovery research`.
- Do not promote a candidate automatically.
- Do not build the multi-round improvement loop in this phase.
- Do not create a new Wide2 baseline unless Wide v1 results prove unusable.
- Do not overwrite the existing wide baseline strategies.
- Do not change core backtest, runner, GUI, or strategy execution code.

## Why `min_samples=30`

The previous optimized tick CSV had about 100 trades, so `min_samples=30` was too restrictive for candidate generation.

The Wide v1 CSV has:

```text
40,937 trades
```

Therefore `min_samples=30` is appropriate again. It should reduce noisy tiny buckets while still allowing many candidate expressions.

## Decision Cases

### Case A: at least one candidate passes promotion

```text
next step:
  discovery promote / WFO final validation
```

### Case B: best_candidate exists but promotion fails

```text
next step:
  analyze failure reasons
  document candidate improvement direction
  decide whether to design v2 loop
```

### Case C: most candidates are fallback candidates

```text
next step:
  increase candidate_pool_multiplier
  inspect why estimated_retention filters out most candidates
```

### Case D: insufficient_retention_candidates

```text
next step:
  allow fallback
  increase candidate_pool_multiplier
  or lower min_estimated_retention only after reviewing trade-count risk
```

### Case E: no_expressions

```text
next step:
  lower min_samples
  increase top_n/candidate_pool
  inspect CSV column availability
```
